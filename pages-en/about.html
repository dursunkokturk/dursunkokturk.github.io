My Educational Journey

My interest in software and computer technologies began during my middle school years. While visiting a bank branch with my father, I became curious about what was happening behind the scenes of the operations performed by bank employees using computers. This curiosity became the first step in my journey into the world of software development.

I turned this curiosity into a professional goal by choosing Computer Programming as my field of study in high school. I continued my education in the same field at the associate degree level. With the goal of becoming an engineer, I transferred to a bachelor's degree program through the Vertical Transfer Examination (DGS) and graduated with a degree in Information Systems Engineering.

Throughout my education, I focused on reinforcing my theoretical knowledge through practical projects and continuously improving my skills in software development.
